# Stockbuild Cut Planner

A free, offline-capable fat-loss planner. Enter your stats and goal length (45, 60, 90 or 120 days) and it builds a day-by-day plan: 16:8 fasting, carb cycling, five short lifting sessions a week, a walk-to-run progression, scaled meals and groceries, and daily tracking with a weekly check-in that adjusts your portions to your real rate of loss.

Everything is saved on your own device; nothing is sent anywhere. Not medical advice.

Open it: https://jacekurtauniversity-wq.github.io/General-Workout-Plan/ then "Add to Home Screen" on a phone.
